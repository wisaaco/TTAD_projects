Gross Turnover for the year grew by 20.2% to Rs. 19505 crores. Net Turnover at Rs. 12369 crores grew by 26.3% driven by the non-cigarette FMCG businesses, higher agri-business revenues and the continuing strong performance by the Hotels business. The non-cigarette portfolio grew by 37.6% during the year and now accounts for 52.3% of the Company’s Net Turnover. Pre-tax profit increased by 20.1% to Rs. 3927 crores, while Post-tax profit at Rs. 2700 crores registered a growth of 20.8%. Earnings Per Share for the year stands at Rs. 7.19. Cash flows from Operations stood at Rs. 3402 crores during the year.
