Figure 3. The image is a line chart representing the ITC share price and volume traded on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) from April 2006 to March 2007. - The left y-axis represents the ITC share price in rupees (Rs.), ranging from 75 to 275. - The right y-axis represents the volume of shares traded, expressed in thousands (000), ranging from 50,000 to 250,000. - The x-axis represents the time period, with data points for each month from April 2006 to March 2007. - There are three lines: - The dark blue line represents the highest share price for each month. - The gray line represents the lowest share price for each month. - The light blue line represents the volume of shares traded for each month. Throughout this period, the chart shows fluctuating trends in both the share prices and the volume traded.
