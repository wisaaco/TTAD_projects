In order to strike a balance between the need to sustain strategic investments for a secure future and the annual expectation of shareholders for growing income, your Directors are pleased to recommend a dividend of Rs. 3.10 per share (previous year Rs. 2.65 per share) for the year ended 31st March, 2007. The cash outflow in this regard will be Rs. 1364.50 crores (previous year Rs. 1134.70 crores) including Dividend Tax of Rs. 198.21 crores (previous year Rs. 139.56 crores). Your Board further recommends a transfer to General Reserve of Rs. 1250 crores (previous year Rs. 1150 crores). Consequently, your Board recommends leaving an un appropriated balance in the Profit and Loss Account of Rs. 647.53 crores (previous year Rs. 562.06 crores).
